Research Foundation

From Software Artifacts to Software Intelligence

Our research is grounded in the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and Software Engineering. We investigate how software artifacts—including requirements, source code, repositories, developer interactions, architectural decisions, and user feedback—can be transformed into actionable engineering intelligence. By combining natural language processing, machine learning, deep learning, foundation models, and agentic AI, we seek to move software engineering from conventional automation toward context-aware, evidence-driven, and increasingly autonomous decision-making.

FOUNDATION / 01

Software Engineering for AI

Software Engineering for AI (SE4AI) focuses on engineering reliable, maintainable, and trustworthy AI-enabled software. Our research examines software quality, engineering practices, testing, monitoring, and lifecycle challenges arising when artificial intelligence becomes an integral part of software.

SE4AI AI Systems Trustworthy AI
FOUNDATION / 02

AI for Software Engineering

AI for Software Engineering (AI4SE) applies machine learning, natural language processing, Transformers, and large language models to support software engineering activities, intelligent analysis, developer assistance, and AI-enabled development workflows.

FOUNDATION / 03

Automated Software Engineering

Automated Software Engineering develops intelligent techniques for automating software engineering tasks. Our work covers software issue classification, technical debt analysis, recommendation, software analytics, and intelligent development support.

FOUNDATION / 04

App Review Analytics

App Review Analytics mines user reviews to identify quality concerns, feature requests, sentiment, sarcasm, user needs, and other actionable signals that can support software evolution and evidence-based product decisions.

FOUNDATION / 05

EHealth Analytics

EHealth Analytics explores the use of artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data analytics to extract meaningful insights from healthcare information and support intelligent, data-driven decision-making in digital health environments.

Electronic Health Records research investigates AI and NLP techniques for analysing structured and unstructured health information, enabling knowledge extraction, healthcare analytics, information access, and intelligent decision support.
