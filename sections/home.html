AISEL AI-Driven Software Engineering Lab

Advancing Intelligent Software Engineering Through AI.

AISEL explores the convergence of Artificial Intelligence and Software Engineering to transform how software is understood, developed, and evolved. Through Automated Software Engineering (ASE), data-driven software repository intelligence, and Natural Language-based Software Engineering (NLbSE), we develop AI systems that learn from software artifacts, understand engineering context, and turn software data into actionable knowledge. Our research aims to advance trustworthy and human-centered AI toward intelligent systems capable of assisting, reasoning, and autonomously performing complex software engineering tasks.

AISEL RESEARCH PORTFOLIO

Featured Research Areas

AISEL brings together Artificial Intelligence, Natural Language Processing, software engineering, and data-driven analytics to develop intelligent methods for understanding, automating, and improving modern software systems. Our research portfolio focuses on building practical and trustworthy AI technologies that support software engineers across the entire software lifecycle — from requirements and development to quality assurance, maintenance, repository intelligence, and autonomous engineering.

01
RESEARCH AREA / 01

Intelligent & Autonomous Software Engineering

Research on applying Artificial Intelligence to automate and augment software engineering activities across the software development lifecycle. This includes intelligent requirements engineering, code generation and analysis, software testing, defect prediction, maintenance, refactoring, and automated decision-making. The goal is to develop AI-powered software engineering systems that can reason, learn, and autonomously support complex development tasks.

02
RESEARCH AREA / 02

Natural Language-Based Software Engineering

Research at the intersection of Natural Language Processing and Software Engineering, focusing on extracting, understanding, transforming, and generating software-related knowledge from natural language. This includes requirements analysis, app-review mining, semantic similarity, topic modeling, sentiment and sarcasm detection, software documentation, and NLP-driven requirements engineering. The broader objective is to bridge the gap between human language and software artifacts.

03
RESEARCH AREA / 03

Data-Driven Software Repository Intelligence

Research on transforming large-scale software repositories into actionable engineering knowledge through data mining, machine learning, deep learning, and repository analytics. This area investigates source code, commits, issues, pull requests, app reviews, APIs, software architectures, and other software artifacts to discover patterns, predict software outcomes, and support engineering decisions. The focus is on building evidence-driven intelligence from continuously generated software ecosystem data.

04
RESEARCH AREA / 04

AI-Driven Software Quality, Reliability & Trustworthiness

Research on using AI to understand, predict, and improve software quality, reliability, security, maintainability, and trustworthiness. This includes quality concern extraction from user feedback, defect and risk prediction, software quality assessment, trustworthy AI for software engineering, and intelligent identification of quality-related issues. The objective is to develop AI-based mechanisms that make software systems more reliable, secure, maintainable, and trustworthy throughout their lifecycle.

05
RESEARCH AREA / 05

Generative & Agentic AI for Software Engineering

Research on leveraging Large Language Models, Generative AI, Retrieval-Augmented Generation, and AI agents to create next-generation software engineering systems. This includes agentic requirements engineering, automated software artifact generation, code and test generation, architectural reasoning, software knowledge management, and multi-agent collaboration. The central aim is to move beyond conventional AI-assisted tools toward context-aware, reasoning-driven, and autonomous software engineering agents.
